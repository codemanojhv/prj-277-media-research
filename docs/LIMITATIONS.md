# Limitations

- The included 100-article corpus is synthetic, fictional, and only demonstrates the pipeline. It must not be cited as real coverage.
- Imported source selection and article accessibility are incomplete and can bias a sample. RSS/URL access may fail, and the URL collector rejects private/internal hosts and redirects.
- Online search covers only results returned by Google News RSS and Bing News RSS for the submitted query and chosen sample. It is not exhaustive; provider language/date/source behavior can be inconsistent, and local checks reduce but cannot eliminate mismatches. A selected outlet or narrow date range can exclude relevant reports. Publisher pages may block or limit automated access; those records are stored as labeled feed excerpts, not full articles. Search relevance and language detection should be reviewed before drawing conclusions.
- Unicode-script detection does not reliably handle transliteration, code-switching, or short text; its confidence is not statistically calibrated.
- Government relevance phrase matching can miss valid coverage or match an incidental phrase. Review its score and returned reasons.
- Sentiment, stance, topics, entities, claims, concerns, and positive aspects use a local evidence-oriented baseline. Errors, translation issues, ambiguity, and sample size remain material; stance is not inferred from sentiment alone.
- Optional TypeSafe JEV classifications are probabilistic model decisions, not verified facts. The API does not provide quote-level rationales, so the UI shows the full article and keeps locally extracted snippets separate. Confidence is not factual correctness, and multilingual quality needs review with human-labeled articles before academic claims. Enabling JEV transmits collected non-synthetic article text to TypeSafe; synthetic demo text stays local.
- Public reaction sentiment is based on a small, capped sample from Bluesky and Hacker News. Search availability, indexing, headline matching, language, and platform demographics limit coverage. One person can contribute more than one post/comment; each item is one count. Counts do not represent all readers, voters, or the public, and engagement scores do not establish how many people agree. News feeds provide coverage, not audience reactions; Reddit, X, and publisher comment systems need separate credentials or source-specific integrations and may not expose comments for every article.
- Embedding clustering can split one story or merge different events. The lexical fallback is less semantic than the multilingual embedding model and is identified by its model label/log output.
- Coverage comparisons summarize the selected sample only. Different language/outlet sample sizes can create apparent emphasis differences. No output measures objective bias, truth, intent, political support, or overall outlet quality.
- Before publication, researchers should establish a sampling frame, validate each language with human reviewers, and record model versions and correction decisions.
